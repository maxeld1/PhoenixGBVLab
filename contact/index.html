A note

If you're a survivor in crisis

We are a research lab — we are not a crisis service. If you or someone you know is in danger or needs immediate support, please reach out to a trained advocate.

In the United States, the National Domestic Violence Hotline and RAINN both offer 24/7 confidential support.
