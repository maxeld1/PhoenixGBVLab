Kayleigh Izzo

Research Associate

About

Kayleigh Izzo is a researcher in the Phoenix Gender-Based Violence Lab in Philadelphia. Her work focuses on healing after trauma, with an emphasis on translating research into practice and policy to create meaningful change. Passionate about improving the lives of trauma survivors, Kayleigh aims to bridge the gap between research and real-world impact, ensuring that evidence-based approaches inform healthcare, advocacy, and systemic responses to trauma.
